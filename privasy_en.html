Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the LEVERANA Driver application (hereinafter referred to as the "App") handles user information.

1. Collection and Storage of User Data

The LEVERANA Driver App is built with an Offline-First design approach. All personal data, shift logs, fueling entries, cadence records, and other technical parameters entered by the user are saved exclusively locally on your device (in the App's local database). We do not transfer, collect, or store your user data on external servers.

2. Advertising Services (Start.io)

To provide free access to the App, the Start.io (StartApp) advertising module is integrated into it. The Start.io module may automatically collect anonymized technical information to select and display relevant advertisements.

This information may include:

  • IP address and approximate location (country/city-level geolocation);
  • Device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID / AAID);
  • Technical specs of the device (model, OS version, system language);
  • Ad interaction data (views, clicks).

You can review Start.io's privacy policy at the following link: Start.io Privacy Policy.

3. Data Security

We take all necessary measures to protect your data at the Android operating system level. Since all data is stored locally on your device, its security also depends on the physical security of the device itself.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes become effective immediately upon being published on this page.
